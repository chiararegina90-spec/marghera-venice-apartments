💡 Save Energy

Insert the key and turn it as if opening a door. While active, the key stays locked. To leave, turn it back and remove it. It switches off only lights and air conditioning; Wi‑Fi, fridge, sockets, TV and other appliances remain powered.

🏠 Home Comfort

❄️ Hisense air conditioning

3 independent units: living room + 2 bedrooms. Each room has its own wall-mounted remote: use it only for that room. ON/OFF to power, MODE for COOL / HEAT / DRY / FAN / AUTO, ▲/▼ for temperature and FAN SPEED for airflow.

Recommended: 24–26°C in summer, 19–21°C in winter.

🌡️ 35°C outside? Don’t set 16°C

It will not cool faster. The unit is already working at maximum capacity; setting 16°C only makes it run for longer.

🔥 Radiators

Side valve: 0 off · 1–2 low · 3 medium · 4–5 high. When central heating is not active, use the AC in HEAT mode.

☀️ Electric awnings

One awning for each bedroom, each with its own remote. Retract fully before going out and in wind, rain or bad weather.

📺 Smart TVs

55" TV in the living room + Smart TVs in the bedrooms. Netflix, Disney+ and Amazon Prime Video are already available.

🌿 Terrace

🍳 Appliances

🍽️ Dishwasher KL52E100S

Complete detergent + rinse-aid tablets are provided. Put 1 tablet in the dispenser inside the door. ON/OFF → select program → close door. 30 min = fresh soil; 90 min = normal; 120 min = drier soil; ECO = most efficient but longer; Rinse & Hold = rinse only, no tablet. AirDry may open the door automatically at the end.

🧺 SanGiorgio SG710 washing machine

Complete detergent + softener PODS are provided. Put the POD directly in the drum before clothes, NOT in the drawer. Quick 30°C ≈ 40 min; Cotton 40°C ≈ 2 h 20 min. Other programs: Delicates, Wool, Synthetics, Eco 40–60, Drain/Spin.

☕ Nespresso Inissia + moka

Fill the tank, press Espresso or Lungo, wait about 25 seconds, insert a capsule and select Espresso (~40 ml) or Lungo (~110 ml). Lift the lever after brewing. Capsules and ground coffee for the moka are provided.

🔥 Gas hob

Press and turn the correct knob; keep it pressed for a few seconds until the flame stays on. Adjust the flame and return to 0/OFF after use. Never leave it unattended.

🥧 Beko oven

Left knob = function; right knob = temperature. For general cooking use top + bottom heat. The temperature light goes off once the oven reaches the set temperature. Return both knobs to OFF after use.

📡 Microwave

Top knob = power; bottom knob = time and automatic start. DEFROST (kg) for defrosting. No metal and never run empty.

🫖 Kettle & 🍞 toaster

Kettle: do not exceed MAX; it switches off automatically. Toaster available in the kitchen.

♻️ Waste & Recycling

In the kitchen there are 2 large bins: paper and plastic. Under the sink there are 2 more bins: residual/non-recyclable waste and organic waste.
