💡 Save Energy

Insert the key and turn it as if opening a door. While active, the key stays locked. To leave, turn it back and remove it. It switches off only lights and air conditioning; Wi‑Fi, fridge, sockets, TV and other appliances remain powered.

🏠 Home Comfort

❄️ Ducted air conditioning

One control panel next to the entrance door manages the whole apartment. ON/OFF to power; MODE for cooling / heating / dehumidifying / fan / auto; ▲/▼ for temperature.

Recommended: 24–26°C in summer, 19–21°C in winter.

🌡️ 35°C outside? Setting 16°C will not cool fasterThe system is already working at maximum capacity; setting 16°C only makes it run for longer.

🔥 Independent radiators

The thermostat is in the hallway, on the wall between the kitchen and bathroom. Turn the knob to the desired temperature; we recommend about 20–21°C. In the first cool autumn days, the heat pump is usually more efficient while outdoor temperatures are still mild.

🍳 Appliances

🧺 Beko washer-dryer

Complete PODS under the sink. Put the POD directly in the drum before clothes, not in the drawer. Select program/options and Start/Pause. Drying: rack indoors or last 3 lines on the right in the garden; pegs are in the hanging basket.

🔥 Induction hob

ON/OFF → select zone → +/– for power. H = surface still hot; LO = control lock.

📡 LG microwave

Use a microwave-safe container, choose power and time, then start. No metal and never run empty.

🔥 Indesit oven

Left knob = function; middle = timer; right = temperature. Return all controls to 0/off after use.

☕ Nespresso Inissia, moka & kettle

Nespresso: fill tank, press Espresso/Lungo, wait for steady lights, insert capsule and choose size. Capsules provided. Ground coffee for moka is also available. Kettle: do not exceed MAX; automatic switch-off.

There is a shared condominium garden behind the building. Access it through the wooden door in the entrance hall.

  • With pets: always pick up after them.
