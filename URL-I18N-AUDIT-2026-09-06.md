## Fixes made during this audit
1. Added a dedicated locale mapping for the image-credit pages. Previously the Header language selector could build non-existent combinations such as `/de/crediti-immagini`.
2. Removed two Italian ALT-text residues from the English home page.
3. Replaced one visible Italian sentence in the English Dimora Castelli page.
4. Culture narrative city names now use localized city labels instead of raw Italian city names in foreign-language generic narratives.
5. Expanded city-name localization, including French forms such as Vérone, Vicence and Trévise.
6. Added full DE / FR / ES / ZH editorial translations for the nine flagship culture narratives that previously fell back to generic copy: Doge’s Palace, St Mark’s Basilica, Peggy Guggenheim Collection, Murano Glass Museum, Santi Maria e Donato, Santa Maria Assunta Torcello, Scrovegni Chapel, Verona Arena and Teatro Olimpico.

## Translation checks
- Route parity exists across all six languages for Journal and destination-guide pages.

- Culture ticket/opening-hour fallbacks are localized: when a current exact detail cannot safely be retained, the foreign-language page shows a localized instruction to verify the official source rather than leaking Italian source text.
